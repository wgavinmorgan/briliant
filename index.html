Silent Reflections at Misty Dawn

Waking up before 5:00 AM always feels like a gamble with nature, but the stillness of Lake Bled at dawn rewarded every step in the freezing air. The light was soft and monochromatic, barely piercing through the thick low-hanging mist.

I composed this shot using the natural frame of the overhanging branches. The goal was to capture the solitude—the feeling of the world being completely asleep while the water reflects a quiet, timeless morning.
